## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a calm, warm, and restorative domestic environment. Built for households, families, couples, and housemates, it strips away the anxiety, urgency spikes, and sterile ticketing tropes of enterprise productivity tools. The visual voice is compassionate, gentle, and organized—anchored by the philosophy of "Clean tasks, clear minds."

The design movement blends **Warm Minimalism** with **Soft Tactility**:
- Ample negative space that lets domestic life breathe, refusing dense data tables or micro-charts.
- Human, approachable geometry featuring pill-shaped buttons, extra-soft corner radii, and gentle organic containers.
- Calming surfaces tinted with subtle warmth, avoiding harsh monochromatic polar whites and stark clinical grays.
- Physical, domestic metaphors: chore assignment resembles hanging a note on an airy wooden board or clean kitchen fridge rather than filing a corporate Jira ticket.

## Colors

The palette balances grounding aquatic slates with airy skylights and warm porcelain neutrals.

- **Primary (`#355872` - Slate Blue):** Grounded, reliable, and deep without the severity of pure navy or charcoal. Used for primary interactive actions, high-emphasis text, and core structural navigation.
- **Secondary (`#7AAACE` - Soft Denim):** A soothing mid-tone for active states, secondary buttons, progress indicators, and neutral-priority task tags.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#9CD5FF` - Sky Blue):** Light, breezy, and optimistic. Used for subtle highlights, background washes behind selected cards, and low-priority task pills.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7F8F0` - Warm Cream / Alabaster):** The universal background baseline. Imbues every screen with a cozy, domestic linen warmth that relieves screen fatigue.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` tinted with 1% Slate):** Pure elevated cards and sheets sit on crisp, warm-tinted white to establish immediate hierarchy against the canvas.
- **Functional & Priority Accents:**
  - *Success / Completion:* `#6FA287` (Muted Sage Green). Celebratory yet soothing; marks completed chores without harsh electric greens.
  - *Urgent Priority:* `#E27D60` (Warm Coral Amber). Distinct and noticeable without inducing clinical panic.
  - *Medium Priority:* `#7AAACE` (Soft Denim).
  - *Low Priority:* `#9CD5FF` (Muted Sky).

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies. Its rounded apertures, friendly terminal curves, and clean geometric foundations provide the ideal intersection between effortless readability and modern domestic friendliness.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in Medium to Semi-Bold weights with subtle negative tracking. This tightens header compositions, preventing them from feeling loud or clinical.
- **Body & Task Copy:** Uses generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to ensure chore descriptions, shared grocery items, and household reminders can be skimmed effortlessly while multi-tasking around the home.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Kept in higher-contrast Slate Blue or Muted Slate (`#5A6F7F`) with slightly relaxed letter spacing for instant mobile clarity on small screens and kitchen tablets.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on **Breathing Room and Contextual Focus**:
- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - *Mobile / PWA Viewports (< 640px):* Single-column flow with `1rem` edge margins and full-width touch-friendly task cards.
  - *Tablet Viewports (640px – 1024px):* 6-column fluid grid, optimized for kitchen counter mounts and split-screen viewing.
  - *Desktop Viewports (> 1024px):* 12-column fixed-max layout capped at `1200px` centered width. Rather than expanding infinitely to look like an admin tool, the extra viewport margins frame the app like a curated household bulletin board.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Elements are grouped in clear cognitive islands using `space-md` (`16px`) between related list items and `space-xl` (`36px`) between category sections (e.g., "Morning Routines" vs "Weekly Maintenance").

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids rigid borders and aggressive drop shadows, utilizing **Tinted Ambient Glows** and **Tonal Layering**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** Tone `#F7F8F0`. Provides warmth without glare.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Lists):** Pure surface white with a diffused, slate-tinted ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(53, 88, 114, 0.06)`. Cards feel lightly floated, soft, and touchable.
- **Level 2 (Active Drag, Hover, or Selected Item):** Slightly elevated with a doubled blur: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(53, 88, 114, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Task Creation Drawers, Bottom Sheets):** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(53, 88, 114, 0.18)` accompanied by a gentle frosted backdrop (`rgba(247, 248, 240, 0.75)` with `8px` blur) that softens background distraction without turning the screen pitch black.

## Shapes

The system relies on generous, organic radii to enforce approachable ergonomics and eradicate harsh mechanical corners:

- **Standard Elements (Cards, Containers, Input Fields):** Standardized on `rounded-lg` (`16px`) and `rounded-xl` (`24px`).
- **Pill Geometry:** Used for user avatar badges, priority pills, filter tags, and quick-add actions, lending a tactile, playful touch.
- **Interaction Feedback:** Rounded states scale inwards gently during active taps, reinforcing a soft rubberized feel appropriate for touch-first PWA usage.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#355872` (Slate Blue) with white text, fully rounded pill (`9999px`) or `16px` radius, `12px 24px` padding. On hover/active, transitions to a soft deep tone with a subtle upward spring.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF` with a 1.5px border tinted in `#7AAACE` and `#355872` text.
- **Quick-Add Floating Action Button (FAB):** Pill or circle shape in `#355872` featuring a clear `#9CD5FF` accent plus icon, fixed for seamless one-handed thumb interaction on mobile devices.

### Task Cards
- Rounded corners (`20px`), solid `#FFFFFF` surface, ambient slate shadow.
- Inner layout consists of a left-aligned friendly checkbox, task title in Semi-Bold, assigner/assignee soft circular avatar, and a muted due-date pill.
- Swipe gestures on mobile reveal a soft sage green reveal zone for completion or warm cream for rescheduling.

### Checkboxes & Completion Mechanism
- Replaces standard square micro-checkboxes with generous `24px` rounded circles.
- Unchecked: 2px border in `#7AAACE` with a warm white center.
- Checked: Animated fill into `#6FA287` (Muted Sage) with a gentle checkmark stroke. Triggers a mild text strikethrough in `#7AAACE` with no harsh jarring screen shakes.

### Input Fields
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` or translucent tint of `#F7F8F0`.
- Border: `1.5px` border in `#E1E6D8` (warm neutral border), focusing smoothly to `#7AAACE` with a diffused glow ring. Radius set to `14px`.
- Generous padding (`14px 18px`) for effortless finger-tap accuracy.

### Chips & Badges
- **Assignee Avatar Chip:** Micro-pill with user thumbnail, name, and soft tint background corresponding to that household member's personal color accent.
- **Priority Badges:**
  - *Urgent:* Background `rgba(226, 125, 96, 0.15)`, text `#C65A3C`.
  - *Normal:* Background `rgba(122, 170, 206, 0.18)`, text `#355872`.
  - *Low:* Background `rgba(156, 213, 255, 0.25)`, text `#2B587B`.

### Household Progress Pill
- A cozy progress indicator placed at the top of the daily view displaying "Roommate / Family Harmony": a smooth, thick pill bar (`12px` height) with a soft Sage Green fill tracking daily chore completion collectively rather than competitively.
